About this app

Brawl Toolbox is an unofficial companion app for Brawl Stars players. It shows the live event rotation, every brawler with their star powers and gadgets, every map, your own trophies and recent battles, and a quiz to pass the time between matches.

This material is unofficial and is not endorsed by Supercell. For more information see Supercell's Fan Content Policy. Brawl Stars and all related names and images are trademarks of Supercell Oy.

Common questions

Where do I find my player tag? Open Brawl Stars and tap your name at the top left. The tag sits under it and starts with #. Entering it is optional — the rest of the app works without one.

My stats say the tag doesn't exist. Tags use the digit zero, never the letter O, and the letters I and L are easy to mix up. Copy it straight from the game rather than typing it from memory.

My trophies look out of date. Supercell's API updates a short while after a match ends, not instantly. Pull down on the My stats tab to refresh.

A brawler has no picture. Artwork for the newest brawlers is sometimes published a few days after they are added to the game. Their name and stats still work in the meantime.

The event rotation looks wrong. Events change on the hour. If a card says it is ending, reopening the app will pull the new rotation.

Nothing loads. The app needs an internet connection. If the game's servers are in maintenance, player lookups will fail until Supercell brings them back.
