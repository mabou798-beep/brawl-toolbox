Privacy Policy

Brawl Toolbox · Last updated 21 October 2026

SupportPrivacy Policy

Summary

Brawl Toolbox has no accounts and no sign-in. We never ask for your name, email address or any other personal detail, and we run no database that stores information about you. The only data leaving your device goes to the third-party services listed below.

Your player tag

The My stats tab asks for your Brawl Stars player tag — the public code shown under your name in the game, such as #2PP0GL9. It is optional: every other part of the app works without it.

If you enter one, it is saved on your device so you don't have to type it again, and it is sent to Supercell's official Brawl Stars API each time your profile is refreshed, so the game can return your trophies, brawlers and recent battles. Those requests pass through a small forwarding service we operate on Cloudflare, which exists only because Supercell's API requires a fixed server address. That service keeps no record of who asked for what; it briefly caches the game's replies so repeated lookups are faster.

You can remove the tag at any time with Use a different tag on the My stats tab, or by deleting the app.

Information stored on your device

  • Your player tag, if you entered one.
  • Cached images and game data — brawler art, maps and the event rotation are cached so screens open quickly and still work on a slow connection.

Deleting the app removes all of it.

Advertising

The app displays advertising through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and process device information, such as a device advertising identifier, IP address, approximate location derived from it, and ad interaction data, in order to serve and measure ads.

Shortly after first launch the app asks, through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt, whether you allow tracking across apps and websites. If you decline, ads are still shown but are not personalised using that identifier, and no feature is withheld. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Google's handling of this data is described in its own policies: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Game data sources

Brawler, map and game mode information, and the artwork shown in the app, are requested from BrawlAPI and its image CDN. The live event rotation and player profiles come from Supercell's official Brawl Stars API. Apart from the player tag described above, these requests contain no information about you — though, like any internet request, the receiving service can see your IP address.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Your choices

  • Use the app without entering a player tag at all.
  • Remove a saved tag with Use a different tag on the My stats tab.
  • Decline or revoke app tracking in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Delete the app to remove everything it stored on your device.
